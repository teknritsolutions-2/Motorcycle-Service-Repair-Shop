Service Capability & Independence Notice

Service capability varies by specific model, year, parts availability, and the nature of the mechanical or electrical fault. If you are uncertain whether we can service your specific motorcycle or acquire appropriate parts, please contact the workshop before bringing your bike in.

How We Approach Different Makes

Correct Fluid Specifications

We match manufacturer oil viscosity (10W-40, 15W-50, etc.), JASO MA/MA2 motorcycle wet-clutch standards, and correct DOT brake fluids.

Torque Specs by the Book

Caliper bolts, axle nuts, pinch bolts, and spark plugs are tightened to correct torque ratings using calibrated torque wrenches.

Quality Replacement Parts

We source reliable OEM or reputable aftermarket parts (filters, sprockets, pads, chains) and discuss options with you first.
