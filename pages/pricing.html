Cost Transparency

What Affects the Final Price?

We believe riders deserve to know exactly why repair costs vary between different bikes.

Engine Size & Fluid Volume

A 150cc commuter uses under 1 litre of oil, while a 650cc twin or large single may require 2.5 to 3.5 litres of synthetic motorcycle oil plus special filter cartridges.

Fairings & Access Complexity

Fully faired sports tourers require significant careful panel disassembly before battery, spark plugs, or oil filters can be accessed, adding labor time compared to naked bikes.

Seized Hardware & Corrosion

Motorcycles ridden daily in wet weather or stored outdoors often have rusted axle adjusters, frozen caliper pins, or corroded electrical terminals that demand careful extraction.

Part Specification Choice

Riders can choose between factory OEM components, high-mileage standard replacements, or upgraded brake compounds/sealed O-ring chains according to their budget.

Common Questions

Pricing & Billing FAQ

Straightforward guidance on how scope and estimates should be confirmed.

The motorcycle should be inspected before the service scope is finalized. Agreed labour and expected parts can then be itemized, with any additional findings presented for approval before extra work proceeds.
Do not assume parts are included. Oil, filters, spark plugs, brake components and other model-specific items should be identified separately in the estimate before work begins.
Acceptance depends on compatibility, condition and the workshop's assessment. Confirm the exact part or fluid before the visit; no warranty or installation policy is implied by this demonstration site.
Diagnostic time and method depend on the symptom, motorcycle and available equipment. The initial investigation scope and any further testing should be agreed before diagnostic work continues.
