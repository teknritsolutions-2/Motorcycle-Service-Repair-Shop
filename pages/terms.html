Workshop Agreement

Terms of Service

General conditions governing service appointments, repair estimates, parts installation, and motorcycle collection.

Effective date: Current published version

This page is a general content framework for the static demonstration build and is not legal advice. The operating workshop should replace it with approved terms that match its actual services, location and consumer-law obligations.

1. Service Appointments & Requests

The current form prepares a service request only; it does not create a confirmed appointment or transmit data to a workshop system. A preferred date and time require separate workshop confirmation.

2. Vehicle Check-In & Physical Inspection

Actual check-in and inspection procedures should be explained by the workshop before work begins. Service scope depends on the motorcycle, the reported issue and physical findings.

3. Estimates & Additional Repairs

Website descriptions are not price quotations. Parts, materials, diagnostic time and labour should be included in an estimate and any changes to the agreed scope should be discussed before additional work proceeds.

4. Customer-Supplied Components

Whether customer-supplied parts can be installed depends on their condition, compatibility and the workshop's actual policy. Acceptance, responsibility and any workmanship terms must be confirmed for the specific job.

5. Completion, Payment & Collection

Completion timing, available payment methods, collection arrangements and any storage conditions have not been supplied for this demonstration. Confirm these directly with the operating workshop.

6. Independent Workshop Status

Apex Motowerks operates as an independent service facility. We are not an authorized agent, franchise, or affiliate of any motorcycle manufacturer. Manufacturer trademarks and model names are utilized solely to indicate vehicle compatibility.
