Privacy Policy

How MotoWorkshop collects, uses, and safeguards your contact details, motorcycle data, and service records.

Effective Date: October 2024

At MotoWorkshop, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us when booking workshop appointments, requesting service estimates, or visiting our workshop facility.

1. Information We Collect

To provide motorcycle servicing, maintenance, and repairs, we collect only the necessary information to communicate with you and maintain your vehicle:

  • Contact Information: Name, telephone number, and email address.
  • Motorcycle Specifications: Make, model, manufacture year, odometer/mileage, registration plate, and reported vehicle issues or service history.
  • Service Records: Invoices, parts fitted, diagnostic logs, and inspection checklists generated during workshop service.
  • Website Preferences: Local storage settings used solely to remember your chosen theme (dark/light mode) and reading direction (LTR/RTL).

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly for legitimate workshop business purposes:

  • To schedule and confirm your motorcycle service appointments.
  • To communicate inspection findings, parts estimates, and vehicle completion status.
  • To maintain vehicle service records so future maintenance recommendations are accurate.
  • To respond to your inquiries submitted through our website or direct contact channels.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or vehicle information with external marketing companies. Information is shared only with:

  • Authorized parts suppliers or specialist machine shops when ordering vehicle-specific replacement parts or machining operations.
  • Statutory vehicle inspection authorities where mandatory roadworthiness reporting is required by local vehicle regulations.

4. Storage & Security

We implement standard organizational and physical safeguards to prevent unauthorized access, loss, or alteration of customer contact records and workshop work orders.

5. Your Rights

You may at any time request to review, update, or request the deletion of your customer contact record from our workshop active files by contacting us directly at service@motoworkshop.local or in person at our workshop desk.

6. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect operational or regulatory changes. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
