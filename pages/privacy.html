Privacy Policy

How MotoWorkshop collects, uses, and safeguards your contact details, motorcycle data, and service records.

Effective date: Current published version

This page is a general website notice for the static demonstration build and is not legal advice. It should be reviewed and replaced with the operating workshop's approved policy before collecting customer data.

1. Information We Collect

The current static website does not send the appointment form to a server. Information typed into the form stays in the browser for the current session unless a future site owner connects a submission service.

  • Website Preferences: Local storage settings used solely to remember your chosen theme (dark/light mode) and reading direction (LTR/RTL).
  • Form Fields: Contact and motorcycle details are entered locally to demonstrate a service-request workflow; the current build does not transmit them.

2. How We Use Your Information

If a submission provider is added later, the workshop should publish who receives the information, why it is needed, how long it is retained, and how a person can request access or deletion.

  • Typical purposes may include responding to a request, discussing inspection findings, and arranging a workshop visit.
  • No future data collection practice should be inferred from this demonstration page.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

The static build does not send form entries to the workshop or a third party. Any future form service, analytics tool, embedded map, or customer-record system must be disclosed in an updated policy.

4. Storage & Security

No claim is made here about an operating business's record systems. Appropriate security and retention controls must be documented when real data collection is enabled.

5. Your Rights

You may request to review, update, or delete contact information by using the workshop's published contact channel once those verified details are available.

6. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect operational or regulatory changes. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
