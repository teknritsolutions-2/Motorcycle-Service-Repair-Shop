Our Services

Everything your motorcycle needs.

From routine checks to complex diagnostics — we cover the maintenance and repair work that keeps your bike running well.

Routine Servicing

General inspection covering fluids, fasteners, tyres, brakes, chain and battery. Keeps your motorcycle in check between major services.

Learn More

Engine Oil & Filter Change

Engine oil drainage and replacement with filter change. Includes leak inspection and oil level verification.

Learn More

Tyre Replacement

Tyre inspection, wear assessment, replacement and pressure check. Valve and rim condition also inspected.

Learn More

Brake Servicing

Pad and disc inspection, fluid check, lever adjustment and caliper assessment. Keeps your stopping power consistent.

Learn More

Chain Adjustment & Lubrication

Chain tension, cleaning, lubrication and sprocket condition check. Essential for drivetrain longevity.

Learn More

Battery Testing & Replacement

Battery voltage and load testing, terminal inspection, charging system check and replacement recommendation.

Learn More

Engine & Electrical Diagnostics

Fault code reading, electrical testing, sensor inspection and systematic investigation of engine and electrical issues.

How It Works

Booking your service is straightforward.

1

Choose Your Service

Browse our services and select the type of maintenance or repair your motorcycle needs.

2

Request a Date

Fill out the appointment form with your preferred date and motorcycle details.

3

Bring In Your Motorcycle

Drop your motorcycle at the workshop on your confirmed date. We'll take it from there.

4

Review & Collect

We'll share our findings and recommendations. Once approved, we complete the work.
