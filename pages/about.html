About the Workshop

A workshop built around getting the work right.

We focus on practical motorcycle maintenance and repair — the kind of work that keeps everyday motorcycles running reliably.

Our Approach

Straightforward motorcycle service.

This workshop exists to do one thing well: maintain and repair motorcycles. We work on commuters, touring bikes, sport bikes and everything in between. Every motorcycle that comes through gets the same level of attention.

We don't rush inspections to move volume. We take the time to check things properly, explain what we find, and make sure the rider understands what's happening with their motorcycle before any work begins.

Inspection Approach

What our technicians check.

During a routine service, we work through the core systems of your motorcycle methodically.

Engine oil level and condition
Brake pad thickness and response
Tyre tread depth and pressure
Chain tension and lubrication
Battery voltage and terminals
Lighting and signals
Coolant level (where applicable)
Throttle and clutch cables
Fastener and bolt condition
General visual condition

Why It Matters

Regular maintenance protects your ride.

Motorcycles are exposed to vibration, heat, weather and road conditions that gradually affect components. Oil breaks down, brake pads wear, chains stretch and tyres lose tread.

Catching these things during a routine check is far simpler and cheaper than dealing with failures on the road. A well-maintained motorcycle is safer, more reliable and holds its value better over time.
