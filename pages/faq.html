Frequently Asked Questions

Practical answers about booking appointments, service intervals, pricing estimates, and what to expect when visiting our workshop.

Service intervals depend on your motorcycle manufacturer, engine displacement, and how the bike is ridden. Most modern street motorcycles require routine engine oil and general checks every 4,000 to 6,000 km (or at least once a year). Bikes used for daily stop-and-go commuting, dusty conditions, or short trips benefit from more frequent oil and chain inspections. Always consult your motorcycle's owner manual for factory-mandated schedules.
You can book individual standalone jobs at any time. Whether you only need a chain clean and tension adjustment, new front brake pads fitted, tyre replacement, or an engine oil change, we handle targeted jobs just as carefully as comprehensive periodic services.
Yes. We regularly work on everyday single and twin-cylinder motorcycles from Honda, Yamaha, Suzuki, Kawasaki, KTM, Royal Enfield, TVS, Bajaj, Hero, and BMW Motorrad. However, service capability can vary based on specific model years, proprietary tooling requirements, and replacement parts availability. If you ride a rare or vintage motorcycle, please contact us first so we can verify parts supply.
Yes. We diagnose check-engine indicators, ABS warning lamps, charging faults, weak starting conditions, and wiring harness gremlins. We use multi-brand diagnostic scan tools, digital multimeters, and battery conductance testers. Note that we do not claim dealership-level proprietary reprogramming for every brand, but we provide structured electrical fault tracing.
We are happy to fit customer-supplied parts (such as aftermarket brake pads, tyres, chains, or exhaust slip-ons) provided the components are brand new, undamaged, and verified as correct for your motorcycle make and year. Our workshop warranty applies solely to our installation labor in these cases, not to the parts themselves.
We provide an initial verbal or written estimate when you drop your motorcycle off. If additional work or replacement parts are discovered to be necessary during physical inspection, our technicians will pause, phone or message you with the exact cost breakdown, and wait for your explicit approval before carrying out any extra work.
Yes, our online booking form allows you to select your preferred date and time slot. Please note that online submissions are appointment requests that must be confirmed by the workshop based on technician capacity and bay availability. We contact you via phone or email to confirm your exact drop-off window.
Previous service invoices or service book stamps are very helpful, especially if you have recently bought the motorcycle used. Knowing when valve clearances were last checked, when brake fluid was flushed, or when fork oil was replaced allows us to avoid duplicating work and focus on what is genuinely due.
Quick services like chain adjustments, battery conductance tests, or standard oil changes can often be completed while you wait if booked in advance for a dedicated slot. For periodic services, comprehensive overhauls, or diagnostic troubleshooting, bikes are typically dropped off in the morning and collected late afternoon once final road-testing and quality checks are finished.
We inspect critical safety components during every service. If we spot worn brake pads, cracked tyre sidewalls, leaking fork seals, or loose steering bearings, we will document the issue, explain the safety implications, provide an estimate, and let you decide whether to address it immediately or defer it for a future visit. We never proceed with unauthorized repairs.
