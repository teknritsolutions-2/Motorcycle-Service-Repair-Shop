Frequently Asked Questions

Practical answers about booking appointments, service intervals, pricing estimates, and what to expect when visiting our workshop.

Service intervals depend on the manufacturer, model, age, mileage and operating conditions. Follow the schedule and specifications in the motorcycle's owner manual, and seek model-specific advice when use or condition suggests an earlier inspection may be sensible.
The service request includes individual jobs such as chain care, brake servicing, tyre replacement and oil changes as well as broader maintenance. Actual availability and scope require workshop confirmation.
The site lists Honda, Yamaha, Suzuki, Kawasaki, KTM, Royal Enfield, TVS, Bajaj, Hero and BMW Motorrad as commonly considered makes. Capability still varies by model year, tooling, fault and parts availability, so the exact motorcycle should be checked before a visit is confirmed.
Warning lights, charging faults, starting problems and damaged connections can be investigated with structured electrical checks. Diagnostic scope and any diagnostic charge should be confirmed before work begins because access and tooling vary by motorcycle.
Customer-supplied parts need to be assessed for condition, compatibility and safety before the workshop agrees to fit them. Acceptance and any workmanship terms should be confirmed for the specific job; bringing a part does not guarantee it can be installed.
An initial estimate can be prepared after the motorcycle and requested work are reviewed. If inspection changes the scope, the revised labour and parts estimate should be agreed before additional work proceeds.
The demonstration form lets you select a preferred date and time window, but it does not transmit the request or create an appointment. Availability and the exact drop-off window must be confirmed through a verified workshop contact channel once one is published.
Confirm the tyre size, intended use, preferred tyre type and current stock availability with the workshop. Final recommendations should follow the motorcycle manufacturer's specifications and an inspection of the wheel, valve and existing wear pattern.
Service duration depends on inspection findings, workshop capacity, parts availability and the motorcycle's condition. Ask for a collection estimate when the booking is confirmed and wait for the workshop to confirm completion before travelling to collect the bike.
The workshop should explain the finding, its relevance and the revised estimate before adding work to the agreed scope. You can then decide how to proceed, subject to any safety limitations the workshop needs to communicate.
