Service Scope & Checklist

Routine Servicing

A general inspection and maintenance session designed to keep your motorcycle running smoothly between major services. Our technicians methodically work through the key systems of your motorcycle to identify wear, leaks, or developing faults early.

← Back to Services Book This Service

What Is Checked

  • Basic fluid levels inspection (engine oil, brake fluid, coolant where applicable)
  • Fastener and bolt torque check on high-stress chassis points
  • Tyre tread depth, profile condition, and cold tyre inflation pressure
  • Front and rear brake pad wear, rotor condition, and lever/pedal travel
  • Drive chain tension, alignment, sprocket tooth wear, and lubrication
  • Battery terminal condition, voltage check, and charging response
  • General condition inspection: fork seals, suspension movement, and wheel bearings
  • Full lighting and signal inspection (headlamp, brake light, indicators, horn)
  • Throttle and clutch cable free-play and smooth operation

Typical Signs This Service May Be Needed

  • Motorcycle has reached its general service mileage or time interval
  • Subtle changes in handling, braking feel, or chain drive response
  • Preparing for an upcoming long road trip, commute cycle, or seasonal start
  • Bike has been stored or ridden through harsh weather conditions

What May Be Replaced or Adjusted

  • Items requiring replacement are identified during inspection and discussed with the owner before any work proceeds.
  • Common adjustments include chain slack, clutch cable free play, tyre pressures, and throttle cable play.
  • Replacement items such as spark plugs, oil filter, air filter, or brake pads are quoted before fitment.

Service & Inspection Notes

Routine servicing is a general physical inspection and adjustment procedure. It is not a substitute for manufacturer-specified maintenance intervals. Findings, proposed work and parts should be discussed before the service scope is finalized. Completion time depends on motorcycle condition and parts availability.
